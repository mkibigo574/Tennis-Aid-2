Our philosophy

Opening the door to tennis.

Determined to promote our sport, we decided

  • to help those who work without adequate materials,
  • to support those who practice tennis without sufficient or proper equipment,
  • to support those who teach in places where there are no facilities,
  • and to open the door to people without resources, with disabilities, refugees or in a situation of social exclusion.

Tennis promotion

What we do

We have different ways of interacting with coaches and players but our most successful actions are the ones when we visit them and we get to see face to face the problems they have to deal with every day. We also ship boxes with the items they lack the most according to their specific needs.

There is a follow-up in every single case to continue our collaboration and to supervise the progress of each program and player.

Who we help

  • Coaches who promote tennis in villages or primary schools.
  • Players with problems to access the right materials: racquets, clothes, shoes, bags, strings and more.
  • Associations, foundations and daycare centres who use the sport as a tool for social inclusion.

Technical assistance

We offer seminars and courses to coaches to teach them how to work with large groups and maximise the resources they have when conditions are difficult.

Equipment shipments

Clothes, shoes, racquets, tennis bags. Players often replace them when they still can be used. We collect them and make sure that they are delivered to people who need them and end up in good hands.

Social inclusion

  • We support tennis sessions for mental health programs.
  • We promote tennis for people with physical disabilities.
  • We collaborate with daycare, drug and alcohol rehabilitation, and social exclusion centres.
